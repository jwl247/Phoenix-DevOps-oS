--------------------------------------------------------------
KEYS - READ BEFORE ADDING ANY
--------------------------------------------------------------

The phone starts with NO keys on purpose. Phones get lost.
Do not put the full PHOENIX_AUTH on the phone.
Wait for the read-only key, then add it inside Debian with:

mkdir -p ~/.phoenix
nano ~/.phoenix/kernel.env
chmod 600 ~/.phoenix/kernel.env

(kernel.env holds lines like:  PHOENIX_WORKER_URL=https://...   PHOENIX_AUTH=...)


Termux must come from F-Droid (f-droid.org), not the Play Store.
