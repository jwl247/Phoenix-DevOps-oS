federal set-asides

Real contract dollars most small businesses never apply for

Set-aside categories exist because Congress decided small businesses need a real shot at federal work, not just the biggest bidders. The eligibility bar is often lower than people assume — the part that stops most businesses is the application itself: SAM.gov registration, certification narratives written to hold up under review, and compliance documentation that has to stay current after you're certified, not just at signup.

confidentiality

A certification file has your ownership structure, your financials, sometimes your personal history. We handle it the way the SBA review itself will: on the record, access limited to the people working your file, and never shared beyond what the application legally requires. If we wouldn't put it in the filing, we don't ask you for it.

categories

HUBZone

Your business is based in a designated Historically Underutilized Business Zone, and enough of your workforce lives there too.

8(a) Business Development

Socially and economically disadvantaged ownership — a 9-year program with real set-aside and sole-source contract access.

Woman-Owned (WOSB)

At least 51% owned and controlled by women, in industries where women-owned businesses are underrepresented.

Economically Disadvantaged (EDWOSB)

WOSB status plus documented personal financial need — opens a further layer of set-aside contracts.
