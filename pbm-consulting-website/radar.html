how it works

Built for people who'd rather be working

  1. 1

    Matched three ways

    Your NAICS codes, the set-aside programs your certifications qualify you for (small business, WOSB, EDWOSB, HUBZone, 8(a), SDVOSB), and your states. A bid has to fit all three.

  2. 2

    Ten seconds to read

    Each bid shows the program, the agency, the deadline, the place, and a link straight to it on SAM.gov. The bottom line tells you exactly how many notices were checked, so you know it looked.

  3. 3

    Not certified yet? Still useful.

    Tell us which certifications you're working toward and Radar shows the bids you'd be eligible for once you have them. It's the plainest answer to "is certification worth it for me?"
