privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-22. This is a working draft pending attorney review — not final legal terms.

What we collect

When you use the form on this site, we collect your name, business name, business email, and phone number (if provided). If you become a client, your certification file may also include ownership structure, financial information, and other details required by the federal certification you're applying for.

How we use it

Your contact information is used to verify your submission is a real business (via the one-time email code), to reach you about your eligibility, and — if you become a client — to prepare your certification filing. We do not sell your information to third parties.

How we protect it

Access to your file is limited to the people working it. We use Cloudflare's infrastructure (Workers, D1, Turnstile) to run this site and store submissions; verification codes are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing hello@pbmconsultingservice.com, unless we're required to retain it as part of an active federal filing.
