# Phoenix — Laurie's Guide

*This is the plain-text version. In the dashboard, the **GUIDE** tab is a
conversation — ask it anything in your own words and it walks you through it
one step at a time. It's easier than it sounds.*

---

## What is Phoenix?

Phoenix is Jerry's operating system for managing files, tools, and projects.
Think of it as a very organised filing cabinet with a control panel on top.

You have a protected share inside Phoenix. Your files live in the
**clonepool** — a safe, versioned storage area where nothing ever gets
deleted or overwritten.

---

## The Control Panel (Dashboard)

When you open the dashboard you see:

- **Left side** — Sector switches and folder slots. Leave the switches alone
  unless Jerry asks you to flip one.
- **Centre** — The main display. Use the tabs along the bottom to switch views.
- **Right side** — Buttons and system gauges. You don't need to touch these.

---

## The Tabs at the Bottom

| Tab | What it does |
|-----|-------------|
| **AI CHAT** | Ask Phoenix anything. Type your question and press Enter. |
| **GUIDE** | This guide — but as a conversation. Start here if you're not sure. |
| **SHELL** | A command window for Jerry. You don't need it. |
| **CLAUDE** | Jerry's AI assistant, full version. For his work, not day-to-day. |
| **MAP** | Browse files and folders. Click a folder to open it. |
| **SECTOR MAP** | Shows which parts of Phoenix are on. Read-only for you. |
| **CODES** | Where Jerry runs scripts. Only use it if Jerry shows you how. |
| **GLOSSARY** | A searchable index of everything in the clonepool. |

---

## Asking a Question

1. Click **GUIDE** (or **AI CHAT**).
2. Type your question in the box at the bottom.
3. Press **Enter**.
4. The answer appears above.

If it's slow, wait about 30 seconds — it will catch up. Nothing is broken.

---

## Finding a File

1. Click the **MAP** tab.
2. Use the buttons along the top (HOME, DOCUMENTS, DESKTOP…) to jump around.
3. Click a folder to open it.
4. Your files are usually in **DOCUMENTS**, or wherever Jerry told you.

---

## Your Protected Share

Your files live in the **clonepool**. Every file gets a unique fingerprint
so it can never be mixed up with another, even if two files share a name.
Nothing is ever deleted from the clonepool. It is a safe place.

---

## If Something Looks Wrong

- **Nothing responds** — wait 30 seconds, try again.
- **A switch won't turn on** — don't force it. Tell Jerry.
- **Red text in CODES** — stop, and tell Jerry what the red text says.

You cannot break Phoenix by clicking around or asking questions. If you're
ever unsure, ask the GUIDE.
