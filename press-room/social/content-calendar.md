## Why this framing

Options are phrased as pain points, not feature names, on purpose — people
vote on instinct against something they recognize, not a marketing
description. Each option secretly maps 1:1 to a real phoenix-office
feature:

| Poll option (pain) | Maps to (feature) |
|---|---|
| Chasing a signature | Sign & seal, two clicks, tamper-evident |
| Same file, 4 formats | Instant file conversion (any format via LibreOffice) |
| Retyping the same info | Secretary drafts the field for you |
| Which version is real | Automatic full version history |

**Built-in follow-up post, once results are in:** "Turns out X% of you said
[winning pain point] — here's how we fixed that." Two posts out of one poll,
no extra drafting needed.
